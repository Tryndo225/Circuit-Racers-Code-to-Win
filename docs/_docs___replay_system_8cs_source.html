2 * @file Docs_ReplaySystem.cs
3 * @brief Documentation entry for the Replay System subsystem.
4 *
5 * @defgroup replay_system Replay System
6 * @ingroup systems
7 * @brief Race replay recording, replay data storage, replay playback, replay camera support, and replay HUD integration.
8 *
9 * @details
10 * The Replay System records vehicle and race state during a race, stores that data in serializable
11 * replay objects, and later reconstructs the race through replay playback.
12 *
13 * Main components:
14 * - ::ReplayManager records replay snapshots during live races.
15 * - ::ReplaySnapshot stores one recorded sample or replay event.
16 * - ::Replay stores an ordered list of snapshots and duration metadata.
17 * - ::ReplayPreviewer plays replay data back by moving a replay car through recorded/interpolated poses.
18 * - ::FloatCamera provides a free-follow camera used during replay viewing.
19 * - ::ReplayRaceOverlaySource adapts replay timing to the generic ::RaceOverlay system.
20 *
21 * Contents:
22 * - @ref replay_system_overview
23 * - @ref replay_system_data_model
24 * - @ref replay_system_recording
25 * - @ref replay_system_playback
26 * - @ref replay_system_camera
27 * - @ref replay_system_api
28 * - @ref replay_system_integration
29 * - @ref replay_system_performance
30 * - @ref replay_system_troubleshooting
31 * - @ref replay_system_versions
32 *
33 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
34 * @section replay_system_overview Overview
35 *
36 * Responsibilities:
37 * - Record live race state into replay snapshots.
38 * - Mark important replay events such as race start, checkpoint, and race end.
39 * - Store replay duration and snapshot data in serializable objects.
40 * - Save a copied replay when a race ends.
41 * - Replay a saved run by instantiating or controlling a replay car.
42 * - Interpolate replay pose, speed, and steering angle over time.
43 * - Update replay car wheel visuals during playback.
44 * - Display checkpoint split popups during replay playback.
45 * - Feed replay timing and completion state into the race overlay.
46 *
47 * Threading:
48 * - Unity main thread only.
49 * - Recording uses FixedUpdate and checkpoint callbacks.
50 * - Playback uses Update and Unity object transforms.
51 *
52 * Important invariants:
53 * - Replay snapshots are ordered by TrackTime.
54 * - ReplayManager records only while recording is active.
55 * - SaveReplay returns a copy so later recording changes do not mutate saved data.
56 * - ReplayPreviewer copies incoming replay data before storing it.
57 * - ReplayPreviewer needs at least one snapshot to play a replay.
58 *
59 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
60 * @section replay_system_data_model Data Model
61 *
62 * ::ReplaySnapshot:
63 * - TrackTime:
64 * Time in seconds when the snapshot was recorded.
65 *
66 * - TrackPosition:
67 * Recorded vehicle world position.
68 *
69 * - TrackRotation:
70 * Recorded vehicle world rotation.
71 *
72 * - LapIndex:
73 * Lap index at the time of the snapshot.
74 *
75 * - VehicleSpeed:
76 * Vehicle speed at the time of the snapshot.
77 *
78 * - SteeringAngle:
79 * Steering angle at the time of the snapshot.
80 *
81 * - Type:
82 * Snapshot type describing whether the snapshot is a regular sample or a replay event.
83 *
84 * Snapshot types:
85 * - ReplaySnapshot::SnapshotType::FixedUpdate:
86 * Regular physics sample.
87 *
88 * - ReplaySnapshot::SnapshotType::Checkpoint:
89 * Snapshot recorded when a checkpoint is passed.
90 *
91 * - ReplaySnapshot::SnapshotType::RaceStart:
92 * Snapshot marking race start.
93 *
94 * - ReplaySnapshot::SnapshotType::RaceEnd:
95 * Snapshot marking race end.
96 *
97 * ::Replay:
98 * - OverallTime:
99 * Overall replay/race time in seconds.
100 *
101 * - Snapshots:
102 * Ordered list of ::ReplaySnapshot values.
103 *
104 * - Duration:
105 * Effective replay duration. If snapshots exist, this is the larger value between OverallTime
106 * and the last snapshot time.
107 *
108 * Replay copying:
109 * - Replay(Replay other) copies OverallTime and the snapshot list.
110 * - Copy() returns a new Replay instance.
111 * - Reset() clears snapshots and resets OverallTime to zero.
112 *
113 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
114 * @section replay_system_recording Recording
115 *
116 * ::ReplayManager records replay data during live races.
117 *
118 * StartReplay:
119 * - Creates a new ::Replay or resets the existing one.
120 * - Clears cached vehicle references.
121 * - Sets recording state to active.
122 * - Subscribes to checkpoint events through ::CheckPointManager.
123 * - Records a RaceStart snapshot.
124 *
125 * FixedUpdate:
126 * - Records regular FixedUpdate snapshots while recording is active.
127 *
128 * Checkpoint snapshots:
129 * - ReplayManager registers a checkpoint callback.
130 * - When a checkpoint is passed, TakeCheckpointSnapshot records a Checkpoint snapshot.
131 *
132 * SaveReplay:
133 * - Records a final RaceEnd snapshot.
134 * - Stops recording.
135 * - Removes checkpoint listeners.
136 * - Returns a copied ::Replay.
137 * - Returns null if recording was not active.
138 *
139 * Snapshot capture:
140 * - The vehicle is resolved lazily with FindFirstObjectByType<VehicleController>().
141 * - Rigidbody is used to read vehicle speed.
142 * - ::DriveTrainController is used to read steering angle.
143 * - ::RaceTimeManager provides current race time.
144 * - ::TrackManager provides current lap index.
145 *
146 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
147 * @section replay_system_playback Playback
148 *
149 * ::ReplayPreviewer plays saved replay data.
150 *
151 * Playback setup:
152 * - SetReplay(Replay) copies replay data.
153 * - Playback state is reset to time zero.
154 * - If no replay data exists, the replay car is cleared.
155 * - If replay data exists, a replay car is created or the previewer transform is used.
156 * - Playback starts automatically when playOnSetReplay is enabled.
157 *
158 * Replay car:
159 * - replayCarPrefab may be instantiated for playback.
160 * - The first snapshot determines the initial replay car pose.
161 * - Rigidbody components on the replay car are made kinematic.
162 * - Rigidbody collisions are disabled.
163 * - Most MonoBehaviours on the replay car can be disabled so recorded data controls playback.
164 * - The replay car's ::DriveTrainController may remain enabled to update wheel visuals.
165 *
166 * Playback update:
167 * - Update advances replay time when playback is active.
168 * - replayTime is multiplied by playbackSpeed.
169 * - If loopReplay is enabled, playback wraps when it reaches the end.
170 * - If loopReplay is disabled, playback stops at the replay duration.
171 * - The replay pose is applied for the current replay time.
172 *
173 * Sampling:
174 * - If replay time is before the first snapshot, the first snapshot is used.
175 * - If replay time is after the final snapshot, the final snapshot is used.
176 * - Otherwise, neighboring snapshots are selected and interpolated.
177 * - Position, rotation, vehicle speed, and steering angle are sampled.
178 * - Steering angle is passed to DriveTrainController::ApplyReplayWheelVisuals.
179 *
180 * Checkpoint display:
181 * - ReplayPreviewer tracks the next checkpoint snapshot.
182 * - When playback crosses a checkpoint snapshot time, it asks ::RaceOverlay to display a split popup.
183 *
184 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
185 * @section replay_system_camera Replay Camera
186 *
187 * ::FloatCamera is used for replay viewing.
188 *
189 * Responsibilities:
190 * - Follow or orbit around a replay target.
191 * - Use a replay car transform as the camera target.
192 * - Provide viewer control without affecting replay playback.
193 *
194 * ReplayPreviewer integration:
195 * - When the replay car is created, the replay target is assigned to ::FloatCamera when available.
196 * - The replay camera should exist in replay scenes where automatic camera targeting is expected.
197 *
198 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
199 * @section replay_system_api Public API Reference
200 *
201 * ::ReplayManager:
202 * - void StartReplay()
203 * Starts recording a new replay, resets previous replay data when needed, subscribes to checkpoint events,
204 * and records a RaceStart snapshot.
205 *
206 * - Replay SaveReplay()
207 * Stops recording, records a RaceEnd snapshot, removes checkpoint listeners, and returns a copied replay.
208 *
209 * ::ReplaySnapshot:
210 * - ReplaySnapshot(float trackTime, Vector3 trackPosition, Quaternion trackRotation,
211 * int currentLap, float vehicleSpeed, float steeringAngle,
212 * ReplaySnapshot::SnapshotType type)
213 * Creates one replay snapshot from recorded vehicle and race state.
214 *
215 * ::Replay:
216 * - Replay()
217 * Creates an empty replay.
218 *
219 * - Replay(float overallTime, List<ReplaySnapshot> snapshots)
220 * Creates a replay from duration metadata and a snapshot list.
221 *
222 * - Replay(Replay other)
223 * Creates a copied replay.
224 *
225 * - float Duration
226 * Gets the effective replay duration.
227 *
228 * - void AddSnapshot(float trackTime, Vector3 trackPosition, Quaternion trackRotation,
229 * int currentLap, float currentSpeed, float steeringAngle,
230 * ReplaySnapshot::SnapshotType snapshotType)
231 * Adds a snapshot and updates OverallTime.
232 *
233 * - Replay Copy()
234 * Returns a copied replay.
235 *
236 * - void Reset()
237 * Clears snapshot data and resets OverallTime.
238 *
239 * ::ReplayPreviewer:
240 * - bool HasReplay
241 * True when a replay with at least one snapshot is loaded.
242 *
243 * - float CurrentReplayTime
244 * Current playback time in seconds.
245 *
246 * - float ReplayDuration
247 * Duration of the loaded replay, or zero when no replay exists.
248 *
249 * - bool IsPlaying
250 * True when replay playback is advancing.
251 *
252 * - bool IsReplayFinished
253 * True when a non-looping replay has reached the end.
254 *
255 * - void SetReplay(Replay newReplay)
256 * Copies replay data, resets playback state, creates the replay car, and optionally starts playback.
257 *
258 * - void SetReplay(Replay newReplay, GameObject carPrefab)
259 * Sets a replay and overrides the replay car prefab.
260 *
261 * - void Play()
262 * Starts or resumes playback.
263 *
264 * - void Pause()
265 * Pauses playback.
266 *
267 * - void Stop()
268 * Stops playback and returns to the start.
269 *
270 * - void ClearReplay()
271 * Clears loaded replay data and optionally destroys the replay car.
272 *
273 * ::ReplayRaceOverlaySource:
274 * - bool TryGetState(out RaceOverlayState state)
275 * Provides replay timing and replay completion data to ::RaceOverlay.
276 *
277 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
278 * @section replay_system_integration Integration Notes
279 *
280 * Race timing:
281 * - ::RaceTimeManager starts replay recording when the race starts.
282 * - ::RaceTimeManager calls ::ReplayManager::SaveReplay when the race ends.
283 * - The saved replay is submitted to ::GameDataManager with the completed race result.
284 *
285 * Checkpoints:
286 * - ::ReplayManager subscribes to ::CheckPointManager checkpoint callbacks.
287 * - Checkpoint snapshots are recorded alongside regular fixed-update snapshots.
288 *
289 * Game data:
290 * - ::GameDataManager stores best replays for custom levels and the practice/test map.
291 * - Replay buttons should check whether a stored replay exists and contains snapshots before entering replay view.
292 *
293 * UI:
294 * - ::ReplayRaceOverlaySource connects replay playback to ::RaceOverlay.
295 * - Replay checkpoint snapshots can trigger split popups during playback.
296 *
297 * Vehicle visuals:
298 * - ::ReplayPreviewer uses ::DriveTrainController::ApplyReplayWheelVisuals to animate wheel steering during replay.
299 *
300 * Camera:
301 * - Replay scenes should contain a ::FloatCamera if automatic replay camera targeting is desired.
302 *
303 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
304 * @section replay_system_performance Performance and GC
305 *
306 * - ReplayManager records one snapshot per FixedUpdate while recording is active.
307 * - Longer races create more snapshots and larger save data.
308 * - Replay objects are copied when saved and when assigned for playback.
309 * - ReplayPreviewer interpolation is linear in normal playback and uses cached snapshot indices.
310 * - Large replay objects increase PlayerPrefs JSON size when saved through ::GameDataManager.
311 *
312 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
313 * @section replay_system_troubleshooting Troubleshooting
314 *
315 * Replay is null after race:
316 * - Check that ::ReplayManager exists in the race scene.
317 * - Check that ::RaceTimeManager::RaceStart called StartReplay.
318 * - Check that ::RaceTimeManager::RaceEnd reached SaveReplay.
319 *
320 * Replay has no snapshots:
321 * - Check that a ::VehicleController exists during recording.
322 * - Check that ::RaceTimeManager and ::TrackManager exist during recording.
323 * - Check that recording is active before FixedUpdate snapshots are expected.
324 *
325 * Replay button refuses to open:
326 * - The saved replay may be null.
327 * - The replay may have no snapshots.
328 * - The replay duration may be zero.
329 *
330 * Replay car does not move:
331 * - Check that SetReplay was called with valid data.
332 * - Check that replayCarPrefab is assigned if a separate replay car should be instantiated.
333 * - Check that playback is active.
334 *
335 * Replay camera does not follow:
336 * - Ensure the replay scene contains ::FloatCamera.
337 * - Ensure the replay car was created successfully.
338 *
339 * Split popup does not appear in replay:
340 * - Check that the replay contains Checkpoint snapshots.
341 * - Check that ::RaceOverlay.Instance exists.
342 *
343 * Replay data is too large:
344 * - Reduce race duration.
345 * - Reduce recording frequency if the system is extended.
346 * - Avoid storing unnecessary fields in snapshots.
347 *
