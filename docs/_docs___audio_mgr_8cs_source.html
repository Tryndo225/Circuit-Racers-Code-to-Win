1/**
2 * @file Docs_AudioMgr.cs
3 * @brief Documentation entry for the Audio Manager subsystem.
4 *
5 * @defgroup audio_mgr Audio Manager
6 * @ingroup systems
7 * @brief Centralized background music and sound-effect playback with pooling, clip reuse for loops, and low-pass control.
8 *
9 * @details
10 * The Audio Manager is implemented by ::SoundManager, which derives from Singleton<SoundManager>.
11 * It provides one looping music AudioSource with an AudioLowPassFilter and a lightweight SFX layer
12 * built from pooled ::SoundManagerHelper instances.
13 *
14 * Non-looping SFX are intentionally not mapped by clip, so repeated one-shot sounds, such as crashes,
15 * may overlap naturally. Looping and continued SFX are mapped by AudioClip, so one active helper is
16 * reused for each looping clip. Continued SFX use a short grace timer and are stopped automatically
17 * unless they are continued again.
18 *
19 * Contents:
20 * - @ref audio_mgr_overview
21 * - @ref audio_mgr_inspector
22 * - @ref audio_mgr_lifecycle
23 * - @ref audio_mgr_usage
24 * - @ref audio_mgr_api
25 * - @ref audio_mgr_integration
26 * - @ref audio_mgr_performance
27 * - @ref audio_mgr_troubleshooting
28 * - @ref audio_mgr_versions
29 *
30 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
31 * @section audio_mgr_overview Overview
32 *
33 * Responsibilities:
34 * - Music playback through a single looping AudioSource.
35 * - Optional low-pass filtering for music through SoundManager::MusicLowPass.
36 * - Pooled SFX playback through SoundManagerHelper objects.
37 * - Overlapping one-shot SFX for short repeated sounds.
38 * - Per-clip reuse for looping and continued SFX.
39 * - Optional AudioMixerGroup routing for music and SFX.
40 * - Camera-position tracking for manager-position sounds.
41 *
42 * Dependencies:
43 * - Generic.Singleton<T> base type.
44 * - ::SoundManagerHelper for pooled SFX playback.
45 * - UnityEngine.Audio for AudioMixerGroup and AudioLowPassFilter.
46 *
47 * Threading:
48 * - Unity main thread only.
49 * - Initialization happens in Awake().
50 * - Pool maintenance and continuation timers are updated in Update().
51 *
52 * Invariants:
53 * - Exactly one ::SoundManager instance should be active.
54 * - Free pooled helpers have IsUsed == false.
55 * - Non-looping SFX return their helper to the pool after playback finishes.
56 * - Looping and continued SFX remain mapped until stopped, expired, or reused.
57 *
58 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
59 * @section audio_mgr_inspector Inspector
60 *
61 * SoundManager fields:
62 * - musicVolume: volume scalar for background music, in range 0..1.
63 * - sfxVolume: global volume multiplier for sound effects, in range 0..1.
64 * - musicMixerGroup: optional mixer group used by the music source.
65 * - sfxMixerGroup: optional mixer group used by SFX helpers.
66 * - continueThreshold: time in seconds for which a continued SFX remains active
67 * after the last ContinueSFXClip call.
68 *
69 * Runtime-created objects:
70 * - One AudioSource for background music.
71 * - One AudioLowPassFilter for music filtering.
72 * - One or more SoundManagerHelper objects created on demand for SFX playback.
73 *
74 * SoundManagerHelper state:
75 * - IsUsed marks whether the helper is currently reserved by the pool.
76 * - Target is followed every frame when assigned.
77 * - Clip, Volume, Pitch, Loop, SpatialBlend, and OutputMixerGroup are applied to the internal AudioSource.
78 *
79 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
80 * @section audio_mgr_lifecycle Lifecycle
81 *
82 * SoundManager.Awake:
83 * - Enforces the singleton rule through Singleton<SoundManager>.
84 * - Creates the looping music AudioSource.
85 * - Applies the initial music volume and music mixer group.
86 * - Creates the AudioLowPassFilter and initializes it to an open cutoff.
87 *
88 * SoundManager.Update:
89 * - Finds Camera.main when needed.
90 * - Moves the manager object to the main camera position when a camera exists.
91 * - Updates continuation timers for clips registered through ContinueSFXClip.
92 * - Stops and unmaps continued looping SFX whose timers have expired.
93 *
94 * SoundManagerHelper.Awake:
95 * - Creates or reuses an AudioSource.
96 * - Disables play-on-awake.
97 * - Marks the helper object as persistent.
98 *
99 * SoundManagerHelper.Update:
100 * - Follows Target when one is assigned.
101 * - Synchronizes volume, pitch, and spatial blend to the internal AudioSource.
102 *
103 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
104 * @section audio_mgr_usage Usage
105 *
106 * Background music:
107 * @code{.cs}
108 * public class TitleMusic : MonoBehaviour
109 * {
110 * public AudioClip theme;
111 *
112 * private void Start()
113 * {
114 * SoundManager.Instance.PlayMusic(theme);
115 * SoundManager.Instance.SetMusicVolume(0.7f);
116 * }
117 * }
118 * @endcode
119 *
120 * One-shot UI or menu SFX:
121 * @code{.cs}
122 * SoundManager.Instance.PlaySFXClip(clickClip);
123 * SoundManager.Instance.PlaySFXClip(clickClip, 0.8f);
124 * @endcode
125 *
126 * One-shot 3D SFX on a target:
127 * @code{.cs}
128 * SoundManager.Instance.PlaySFXClip(
129 * crashClip,
130 * hitTransform,
131 * volume: 1.0f,
132 * pitch: 1.0f,
133 * loop: false,
134 * spatialBlend: 1.0f,
135 * reset: true);
136 * @endcode
137 *
138 * Looping SFX with per-clip reuse:
139 * @code{.cs}
140 * SoundManager.Instance.PlaySFXClip(
141 * engineLoop,
142 * carTransform,
143 * volume: 0.8f,
144 * pitch: 1.0f,
145 * loop: true,
146 * spatialBlend: 1.0f,
147 * reset: false);
148 *
149 * SoundManager.Instance.PauseSFXClip(engineLoop);
150 * SoundManager.Instance.ResetSFXClip(engineLoop);
151 * SoundManager.Instance.StopSFXClip(engineLoop);
152 * @endcode
153 *
154 * Continued looping SFX:
155 * @code{.cs}
156 * // Refreshes the continuation timer each time it is called.
157 * // The clip stops automatically after continueThreshold seconds without another call.
158 * SoundManager.Instance.ContinueSFXClip(skidLoop);
159 * SoundManager.Instance.ContinueSFXClip(skidLoop, 0.6f);
160 * @endcode
161 *
162 * Music low-pass effect:
163 * @code{.cs}
164 * AudioLowPassFilter lowPass = SoundManager.Instance.MusicLowPass;
165 * lowPass.cutoffFrequency = 1200f;
166 *
167 * // Restore later.
168 * lowPass.cutoffFrequency = 22000f;
169 * @endcode
170 *
171 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
172 * @section audio_mgr_api Public API Reference
173 *
174 * Properties:
175 * - AudioLowPassFilter MusicLowPass:
176 * Provides access to the music low-pass filter.
177 *
178 * Music:
179 * - void PlayMusic(AudioClip clip):
180 * Starts or resumes looping background music for the given clip.
181 *
182 * - void StopMusic():
183 * Stops the current music source if it is playing.
184 *
185 * - void SetMusicVolume(float volume):
186 * Sets the music volume after clamping the value to 0..1.
187 *
188 * SFX:
189 * - void PlaySFXClip(AudioClip clip):
190 * Plays a non-looping SFX at the manager position.
191 *
192 * - void PlaySFXClip(AudioClip clip, float volume):
193 * Plays a non-looping SFX at the manager position with a custom volume.
194 *
195 * - void PlaySFXClip(AudioClip clip, Transform target):
196 * Plays a non-looping SFX following the given target.
197 *
198 * - void PlaySFXClip(AudioClip clip, Transform target, float volume = 1f,
199 * float pitch = 1f, bool loop = false,
200 * float spatialBlend = 0.0f, bool reset = true):
201 * Plays a sound effect with full control over volume, pitch, looping, spatial blend, and reset behaviour.
202 *
203 * Behaviour:
204 * - If loop is false, a free helper is used and the sound may overlap with other plays of the same clip.
205 * - If loop is true, the clip is mapped to one helper and reused.
206 * - If a looping clip is already mapped and reset is true, playback is rewound and restarted.
207 * - If a looping clip is already mapped and reset is false, playback continues when already active.
208 *
209 * - void ContinueSFXClip(AudioClip clip):
210 * Starts or refreshes a looping SFX at the manager position and schedules it to stop after continueThreshold.
211 *
212 * - void ContinueSFXClip(AudioClip clip, float volume):
213 * Same as ContinueSFXClip(AudioClip), but with an explicit volume.
214 *
215 * - void PauseSFXClip(AudioClip clip):
216 * Pauses a mapped looping or continued clip.
217 *
218 * - void ResetSFXClip(AudioClip clip):
219 * Rewinds a mapped clip to the beginning.
220 *
221 * - void StopSFXClip(AudioClip clip):
222 * Stops a mapped clip, removes it from continuation tracking, and releases its helper.
223 *
224 * - void SetSFXVolume(float volume):
225 * Sets the global SFX volume multiplier after clamping the value to 0..1.
226 * Existing one-shot sounds are not retroactively changed.
227 *
228 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
229 * @section audio_mgr_integration Integration Notes
230 *
231 * Vehicle systems:
232 * - Use one-shot SFX for crash sounds so repeated impacts can overlap naturally.
233 * - Use looping SFX or ContinueSFXClip for effects that should persist while a condition remains true.
234 * - Engine audio can be handled separately through EngineSound, while one-shot events may still use SoundManager.
235 *
236 * UI:
237 * - Use PlaySFXClip(AudioClip) or PlaySFXClip(AudioClip, float) for non-positional UI feedback.
238 *
239 * Scene management:
240 * - SceneManagement may call SoundManager::PlayMusic when a scene-specific music clip is matched.
241 *
242 * Audio setup:
243 * - Ensure exactly one active AudioListener exists, usually on the main camera.
244 * - Assign mixer groups when music and SFX should be routed to separate mixer buses.
245 *
246 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
247 * @section audio_mgr_performance Performance and GC
248 *
249 * - SFX helpers are pooled and reused.
250 * - One-shot SFX allocate only when no free helper exists.
251 * - Looping and continued SFX reuse one helper per mapped AudioClip.
252 * - Continued SFX use a short timer instead of starting and stopping every frame.
253 * - Keep continueThreshold small, usually around 0.1 to 0.25 seconds, to avoid unwanted lingering loops.
254 *
255 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
256 * @section audio_mgr_troubleshooting Troubleshooting
257 *
258 * No sound:
259 * - Check that an AudioListener exists.
260 * - Check mixer routing.
261 * - Check musicVolume, sfxVolume, and per-call volume.
262 * - Check that the AudioClip reference is assigned.
263 *
264 * One-shot sound cuts itself off:
265 * - Non-looping SFX should not be mapped. Use loop: false for crashes, clicks, and short impacts.
266 *
267 * Looping sound does not stop:
268 * - Repeated ContinueSFXClip calls refresh the timer.
269 * - Call StopSFXClip manually if the sound should stop immediately.
270 *
271 * 3D sound is not positioned correctly:
272 * - Pass a valid target Transform.
273 * - Use spatialBlend: 1.0f for fully spatialized playback.
274 *
275 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
276 * @section audio_mgr_versions Version History
277 *
278 * - v1.2: One-shot SFX no longer mapped by clip, allowing repeated one-shots to overlap.
279 * - v1.1: Added pooled helpers, per-clip loop mapping, ContinueSFXClip, and MusicLowPass.
280 * - v1.0: Basic music and SFX playback with volume and mixer routing.
281 */
