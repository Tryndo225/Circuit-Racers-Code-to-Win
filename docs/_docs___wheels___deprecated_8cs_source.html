1/**
2 * @file Docs_Wheels_Deprecated.cs
3 * @brief Documentation entry for the deprecated custom wheel controller prototypes.
4 *
5 * @defgroup car_ctrl_deprecated Deprecated Wheel Controller
6 * @ingroup car_ctrl
7 * @brief Legacy experimental wheel physics (kept for reference only).
8 *
9 * @details
10 * This group documents an **experimental, deprecated** attempt to implement a bespoke wheel
11 * solution using mesh colliders and configurable joints. It is **not** used by the current
12 * vehicle system and should not be integrated into new code or scenes.
13 *
14 * Implementations:
15 * - ::Wheel_Collider_3D — a prototype "wheel" built from a child Rigidbody, MeshCollider (convex),
16 * and ConfigurableJoint connected to the car body. Contact points are relayed to apply a crude
17 * forward force based on a pending torque command.
18 * - ::WheelContactRelay — forwards collision contacts to observers via callbacks.
19 * - ::WheelSpec — early data container for wheel assignment (collider/visual/powered/steering).
20 *
21 * Rationale for deprecation:
22 * - Unrealistic tire behavior (insufficient friction model, no pacejka or slip curves).
23 * - Fragile tuning: mass, springs, damping, and joint limits were highly sensitive.
24 * - Complex self-collision filtering and susceptibility to tunneling/instability.
25 * - Unity’s built-in WheelCollider plus the current drivetrain stack provides better stability,
26 * performance, and maintainability.
27 *
28 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
29 * @section car_ctrl_deprecated_overview Overview
30 *
31 * Goals (original):
32 * - Replace WheelCollider with an explicit joint-driven, mesh-collider "wheel".
33 * - Drive traction by applying forces at contact points sourced from collision callbacks.
34 * - Split visuals into steer (yaw) and roll (spin) transforms.
35 *
36 * Actual behavior:
37 * - Contact points are gathered by ::WheelContactRelay and cached each physics step.
38 * - ::Wheel_Collider_3D converts a queued torque value into a forward force at the wheel child’s
39 * position if contacts were detected, then clears the cache.
40 * - Visuals are updated for yaw and spin from the child’s pose and linear speed.
41 *
42 * Limitations:
43 * - No robust slip/adhesion model; force projection is simplistic.
44 * - Requires precise collider filtering to avoid the car colliding with its own wheel.
45 * - Joint/spring tuning is brittle across frame rates and surface setups.
46 *
47 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
48 * @section car_ctrl_deprecated_components Components
49 *
50 * Wheel_Collider_3D:
51 * - Spawns "WheelColliderObject" child with:
52 * - Rigidbody (mass, CCD, interpolation).
53 * - MeshCollider (convex) using a rotated built-in cylinder mesh.
54 * - ConfigurableJoint to the car body (linear limit = suspension travel).
55 * - WheelContactRelay to surface collision contacts.
56 * - Public API:
57 * - ApplyTorque(float): queues torque proxy for next FixedUpdate.
58 * - ApplySteering(float): sets visual yaw angle for the wheel.
59 *
60 * WheelContactRelay:
61 * - Emits Collision.contacts arrays to registered observers each OnCollisionStay.
62 *
63 * WheelSpec:
64 * - Aggregates references used by early controller drafts (collider, visual, flags).
65 *
66 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
67 * @section car_ctrl_deprecated_lifecycle Lifecycle
68 *
69 * Setup:
70 * - On Reset/Awake: creates or validates the child wheel object and components.
71 * - Ignores self-collision between car body collider and wheel MeshCollider.
72 *
73 * FixedUpdate:
74 * - Applies steer yaw to the wheel child.
75 * - If any contacts were reported, applies a forward force proportional to queued torque.
76 * - Spins visual mesh based on child rigidbody linear speed and radius.
77 * - Clears contact cache.
78 *
79 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
80 * @section car_ctrl_deprecated_usage Usage (Not Recommended)
81 *
82 * This subsystem is deprecated and retained only for historical reference. Do **not** integrate it
83 * into production vehicles. Prefer the current drivetrain with Unity WheelCollider and the supported
84 * controllers (see @ingroup car_ctrl).
85 *
86 * Migration guidance:
87 * - Replace Wheel_Collider_3D with standard WheelCollider components.
88 * - Use your active drivetrain/transmission controllers to drive torque, braking, and steering.
89 * - Map visual steering/rolling via WheelCollider APIs and your wheel mesh updaters.
90 *
91 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
92 * @section car_ctrl_deprecated_performance Performance & Stability
93 *
94 * - Mesh collisions on moving wheels are comparatively heavy and prone to jitter.
95 * - Joint-driven suspension without proper constraints can introduce oscillations.
96 * - The simplified traction model leads to non-physical handling at speed and under load.
97 *
98 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
99 * @section car_ctrl_deprecated_troubleshooting Troubleshooting (Historical)
100 *
101 * - Wheel shakes violently: reduce SuspensionDistance, tune Spring/Damper, verify joint axes/limits.
102 * - Car collides with its own wheel: ensure Physics.IgnoreCollision between body and wheel collider.
103 * - No traction: ensure contacts are being relayed; confirm wheel child is not airborne or penetrating.
104 *
105 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
106 * @section car_ctrl_deprecated_versions Version History
107 *
108 * - v0.2 (deprecated): Added contact relay and visual split (steer vs roll).
109 * - v0.1 (experimental): Initial joint/mesh-based wheel prototype.
110 *
111 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
112 * @section car_ctrl_deprecated_status Deprecation Notice
113 *
114 * Status: **Deprecated**
115 * Replacement: Unity WheelCollider + current drivetrain/vehicle controllers in @ingroup car_ctrl.
116 * Intent: Retained for reference and archival documentation only.
117 */
