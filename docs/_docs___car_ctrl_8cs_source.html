2 * @file Docs_CarCtrl.cs
3 * @brief Documentation entry for the Vehicle Control subsystem.
4 *
5 * @defgroup car_ctrl Vehicle Control
6 * @ingroup systems
7 * @brief Modular 4-wheel car stack: input, drivetrain, transmission, audio, lights, VFX, and camera follow.
8 *
9 * @details
10 * The Vehicle Control subsystem is composed of small, focused MonoBehaviours:
11 * - ::VehicleController orchestrates input, delegates to drivetrain/transmission, and wires audio/lights.
12 * - ::DriveTrainContoller applies steering, traction/brake torques, anti-roll, and syncs wheel meshes.
13 * - ::TransmissionController converts wheel RPM to engine RPM and manages auto-shifting with slip gating.
14 * - ::EngineSound synthesizes engine audio from RPM and throttle using 4-band crossfades (on/off layers).
15 * - ::LightsController drives head/day/rear/reverse/brake lights with optional fading and color presets.
16 * - ::TyreEffects spawns skid VFX (particles, trail) and optional skid audio based on slip.
17 * - ::CollisionDetection plays crash SFX using collision impulse with cooldown and layer filtering.
18 * - ::FollowCamera is a simple, stable third-person follow camera with offset and fixed pitch.
19 *
20 * Key design goals:
21 * - Explicit dependencies (Init/SetUp), small clear responsibilities, inspector-first tuning.
22 * - Deterministic physics path (FixedUpdate), minimal GC, predictable audio.
23 *
24 * Contents:
25 * - see car_overview
26 * - see car_scene_setup
27 * - see car_inspector
28 * - see car_lifecycle
29 * - see car_usage
30 * - see car_api
31 * - see car_integration
32 * - see car_performance
33 * - see car_troubleshooting

35 *
36 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
37 * @section car_overview Overview
38 *
39 * Flow (per physics tick):
40 * 1) ::VehicleController reads input (Input System), routes steering/throttle/brakes to ::DriveTrainContoller.
41 * 2) ::DriveTrainContoller queries grounded driven wheels, computes average RPM and slip,
42 * calls ::TransmissionController::HandleShifting(...), applies motor/brake torques and anti-roll forces,
43 * then syncs wheel visuals.
44 * 3) ::VehicleController updates ::EngineSound with current engine RPM and throttle.
45 * 4) ::LightsController is updated for brake/reverse states; manual toggle handles head/day/rear sets.
46 * 5) ::TyreEffects reacts to per-wheel slip and spawns smoke/trails/skid audio.
47 * 6) ::CollisionDetection triggers crash SFX on significant impulses.
48 * 7) ::FollowCamera smooth-follows the target with local-space offset and fixed pitch.
49 *
50 * Physics principles:
51 * - WheelCollider drives traction; forward/sideways friction curves are configurable per axle.
52 * - Anti-roll applies force difference across left/right to resist body roll.
53 * - Traction control reduces motor torque when slip > threshold; ABS reduces brake torque similarly.
54 *
55 * Audio principles:
56 * - Engine audio is 4 bands (idle/low/mid/high) with on/off-throttle layers crossfaded by RPM and load.
57 * - Gear shift "flare" briefly boosts pitch; a soft limiter attenuates near redline.
58 *
59 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
60 * @section car_scene_setup Scene Setup
61 *
62 * Required:
63 * - A GameObject with:
64 * - Rigidbody (non-kinematic).
65 * - 4 WheelCollider components (FL, FR, RL, RR).
66 * - 4 wheel visual Transforms in the same order for mesh sync.
67 * - ::VehicleController, ::DriveTrainContoller, ::TransmissionController, ::EngineSound, ::LightsController.
68 * - Optional:
69 * - ::TyreEffects on each wheel (next to the WheelCollider).
70 * - ::CollisionDetection on the body (requires Rigidbody).
71 * - ::FollowCamera in the scene, target set to the vehicle root.
72 *
73 * Layering:
74 * - Use a "Track" or "Ground" layer for drivable surfaces. Configure ::CollisionDetection ignoreLayers accordingly.
75 *
76 * Audio:
77 * - Route engine and SFX to appropriate AudioMixerGroups. Ensure a single active AudioListener (usually main camera).
78 *
79 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
80 * @section car_inspector Inspector (Main Components)
81 *
82 * ::VehicleController
83 * - wheels[4]: (WheelCollider, wheel visual, powered, steering) in order FL, FR, RL, RR.
84 * - Input actions: throttle, steer, brake, handbrake, lights toggle (auto-create bindings optional).
85 * - Vehicle: center of mass offset, Ackermann factor, max speeds, steering angles/speed, input exponent.
86 * - Motor/Brake: max motor power, max brake torque, handbrake torque.
87 * - Transmission: forward gear ratios, final drive, idle/redline/shift RPMs, shift duration, slip threshold.
88 * - Stability: anti-roll front/rear stiffness.
89 * - TCS/ABS: enable flags and slip limits.
90 * - Friction: per-axle forward/sideways stiffness and curve values.
91 * - Lights: intensities/colors and light lists; fade duration; initial on/off.
92 * - EngineSound: mixer group, volumes, curves, centers, sharpness, on/off clips, shift flare, limiter.
93 *
94 * ::DriveTrainContoller
95 * - Mirrors tuning from VehicleController (SetUp fills these).
96 * - Requires Init(Rigidbody, TransmissionController, WheelCollider[], Transform[], bool[] driven, bool[] steering).
97 *
98 * ::TransmissionController
99 * - forwardGears[], finalDrive, idle/redline, shiftUp/shiftDown RPM, shiftDuration, slipThreshold.
100 * - OnShift: list of callbacks (EngineSound::OnShift is added by VehicleController).
101 *
102 * ::EngineSound
103 * - min/max RPM, on/off clips (idle/low/mid/high), mixer group, volumes, smoothing speeds,
104 * pitch vs RPM curve, band centers/sharpness, on/off balance, shift flare, limiter parameters.
105 *
106 * ::LightsController
107 * - front/day/rear/reverse/brake lights (lists), colors and intensities, fade duration, initial on/off.
108 *
109 * ::TyreEffects (per wheel)
110 * - smoke prefab (ParticleSystem), skid TrailRenderer prefab, skid clip and mixer group.
111 * - slipThreshold, maxEmissionRatePerSecond, maxEmissionRateAtSlip, groundOffset.
112 *
113 * ::CollisionDetection
114 * - crashClips[], volume curve, min pitch/max pitch, cooldown.
115 * - minImpulse/maxImpulse mapping, ignoreLayers.
116 *
117 * ::FollowCamera
118 * - target, local offset, follow speed, yaw smoothness, fixed pitch angle.
119 *
120 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
121 * @section car_lifecycle Lifecycle
122 *
123 * Boot:
124 * - VehicleController::Reset/Start/OnValidate wires missing components, pushes config via SetUp(), and
125 * creates default input bindings if enabled.
126 *
127 * Per physics tick:
128 * - FixedUpdate in VehicleController:
129 * - Read input, call DriveTrainContoller::ApplyWheelControls(throttle, braking, handbrake, steer, isGamepad).
130 * - Update ::LightsController brake/reverse states.
131 * - Refresh engine audio inputs (RPM and throttle).
132 *
133 * Per frame:
134 * - FollowCamera::Update smooths camera.
135 * - EngineSound::Update applies smoothing, band mixing, limiter, and shift flare.
136 * - TyreEffects::FixedUpdate spawns smoke/trails and skid audio based on slip.
137 * - CollisionDetection::OnCollisionEnter plays crash SFX if severity passes threshold.
138 *
139 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
140 * @section car_usage Usage
141 *
142 * Minimal code to spawn and drive:
143 * @code{.cs}
144 * public class Spawner : MonoBehaviour
145 * {
146 * public GameObject carPrefab;
147 * public Transform spawnPoint;
148 *
149 * void Start()
150 * {
151 * var car = Instantiate(carPrefab, spawnPoint.position, spawnPoint.rotation);
152 * // Ensure wheels array and visuals are assigned in prefab's VehicleController.
153 * }
154 * }
155 * @endcode
156 *
157 * Reading vehicle state:
158 * @code{.cs}
159 * var vc = car.GetComponent<VehicleController>();
160 * // Example: hook up UI to RPM
161 * var rpm = car.GetComponent<TransmissionController>().EngineRPM;
162 * @endcode
163 *
164 * Tuning friction (example):
165 * @code{.cs}
166 * // Increase front grip
167 * var dt = car.GetComponent<DriveTrainContoller>();
168 * dt.frontSidewaysFriction[0] = 2.5f; // stiffness
169 * @endcode
170 *
171 * Manual lights:
172 * @code{.cs}
173 * car.GetComponent<LightsController>().ToggleLights();
174 * @endcode
175 *
176 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
177 * @section car_api Public API Reference (Selected)
178 *
179 * ::VehicleController
180 * - void SetUp(): resolves dependencies, pushes inspector config to subsystems.
181 *
182 * ::DriveTrainContoller
183 * - void Init(Rigidbody rb, TransmissionController tx, WheelCollider[] wc, Transform[] meshes,
184 * bool[] driven, bool[] steering)
185 * - void SetUp(): configures wheel substeps, solver iterations, friction, center of mass.
186 * - void ApplyWheelControls(float throttle, bool braking, bool handbrake, float steering, bool gamepadSteering)
187 * - bool Braking (property): true if service brakes active.
188 * - bool Reversing (property): true if reversing mode active.
189 *
190 * ::TransmissionController
191 * - bool HandleShifting(float wheelRPM, float wheelSlip): updates EngineRPM and schedules shift coroutines;
192 * returns true while shifting (torque cut).
193 * - int CurrentGear (property)
194 * - float EngineRPM (property)
195 *
196 * ::EngineSound
197 * - void OnShift(): triggers short pitch flare.
198 * - void SetUp(): clamps and applies inspector parameters to sources.
199 *
200 * ::LightsController
201 * - void ToggleLights()
202 * - void SetBrakeLights(bool active)
203 * - void SetReverseLights(bool active)
204 * - void SetLights(bool active), SetFrontLights(bool), SetDayLights(bool), SetRearLights(bool)
205 *
206 * ::TyreEffects
207 * - Emits particles/trail and plays skid audio automatically based on WheelCollider slip.
208 *
209 * ::CollisionDetection
210 * - Plays one-shot crash SFX based on collision impulse; respects cooldown and layer mask.
211 *
212 * ::FollowCamera
213 * - void SyncCamera(): immediate snap to target position/rotation with fixed pitch.
214 *
215 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
216 * @section car_integration Integration Notes
217 *
218 * Input:
219 * - Uses the Unity Input System. VehicleController can auto-create basic bindings for keyboard/gamepad.
220 * - Device detection switches "gamepad steering" which uses input exponent shaping.
221 *
222 * Audio:
223 * - Connect TransmissionController::OnShift to EngineSound::OnShift (VehicleController does this).
224 * - Use SoundManager (see audio_mgr) to unify SFX routing; CollisionDetection expects SoundManager.Instance.
225 *
226 * VFX:
227 * - Assign TyreEffects prefabs (particles/trails) in the wheel objects; ensure proper layer/materials.
228 *
229 * Camera:
230 * - Assign FollowCamera.target to the vehicle root; tune offset and smoothing for your game feel.
231 *
232 * Networking (out of scope here):
233 * - Keep physics authority on server or host; send inputs and replicate high-level state (gear, RPM, lights).
234 *
235 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
236 * @section car_performance Performance and GC
237 *
238 * - Avoid frequent array reallocation in gameplay; arrays are configured once in SetUp().
239 * - WheelCollider.GetGroundHit is used only when grounded; keep friction and solver iterations reasonable.
240 * - EngineSound pre-creates sources in Awake; clips should be compressed/streamed as appropriate.
241 * - TyreEffects accumulates fractional particles to keep emission stable at fixed delta.
242 * - CollisionDetection uses a short cooldown to prevent SFX spam.
243 *
244 * Suggested physics defaults:
245 * - Rigidbody.solverIterations and solverVelocityIterations set to 12 for stability.
246 * - WheelCollider.ConfigureVehicleSubsteps(0.5, 20, 30) called for each wheel.
247 *
248 * ----------------------------------------------------------------------
249 * @section car_troubleshooting Troubleshooting
250 *
251 * Vehicle will not move:
252 * - Check WheelCollider mass/friction setup and that at least one axle is marked "powered."
253 * - Verify ground colliders and layers; WheelColliders must contact a collider to generate forces.
254 *
255 * Steering feels unresponsive:
256 * - Increase maxSteerAngle and/or steerSpeedDegPerSec; reduce maxSpeed or increase inputExponent.
257 *
258 * Excessive wheelspin:
259 * - Enable tractionControlEnabled and reduce tractionSlipLimit; tune forward friction stiffness/curve.
260 *
261 * Brakes ineffective or lock instantly:
262 * - Enable absEnabled and increase absSlipLimit; adjust maxBrakeTorque and friction curves.
263 *
264 * Audio missing or flat:
265 * - Assign on/off band clips; verify minRPM/maxRPM and pitchVsRpm curve; check AudioMixer routing.
266 *
267 * Lights do not toggle:
268 * - Ensure lists contain valid Light references; verify the lights input binding and that
269 * VehicleController hooks OnLightsPerformed.
270 *
271 * Crash SFX never plays:
272 * - Lower minImpulse, raise baseVolume, ensure cooldown is not constantly active.
273 * - Verify ignoreLayers mask does not include your collision targets.
274 *
