17# Quick Start
181. **Start**
19 - From the main menu, click **Play** or **Test Track**.
20
211. **Start / Load a Track**
22 - Use the level browser to pick a saved map, or generate a new one.
23 - When generating confirm the popup preview to add it to your list or throw it away and generate another one.
24
252. **Grid / Start**
26 - A short countdown appears. When it clears, you’re live.
27
283. **Race**
29 - Follow the **road** to the checkpoints.
30 - Cross **checkpoints** (light blue markers) in order.
31 - Complete the required **laps**. Your **final time** shows at the finish.

35# Controls
36
37## Keyboard & Mouse
38- **Throttle / Brake:** `W` / `S` *(Up/Down Arrow also work)*
39- **Steer Left / Right:** `A` / `D` *(Left/Right Arrow also work)*
40- **Handbrake:** `Space`
41- **Lights (toggle):** `L`
42- **Menus / UI:** Standard mouse navigation
43
44## Gamepad (Xbox / DualShock / Generic)
45- **Throttle / Brake:** Triggers (Right/Left)
46- **Steer:** Left Stick (X)
47- **Handbrake:** South / Cross (`A` on Xbox, `X` on PS)
48- **Lights (toggle):** D-Pad Up
49
50> The game detects your last used device and adapts steering behavior accordingly.

54# HUD & What It Means
55
56- **Start Filter & Timer**
57 A large overlay with a countdown. When it’s gone, your car is live.
58
59- **Lap Time**
60 Current lap’s running timer.
61
62- **Track Time**
63 Total time since the first lap started (hidden until you begin your first lap).
64
65- **Lap Counter**
66 `current / total`, e.g., `2/3`.
67
68- **Checkpoint Counter**
69 `current / total`, showing progress through the lap.
70
71- **Finish Screen**
72 Pops up when the race is complete, showing your **Final Time**.

76# Checkpoints & Respawn
77
78- **Checkpoints**
79 Automatically placed along straights. You must pass them **in order**.
80- **Respawn Timer**
81 If you crash, reset, or the race starts, a short **respawn timer** overlay appears.
82- **Saved State**
83 On checkpoint claim, your **position**, **rotation**, and **velocities** are captured and can be used for fair respawn behavior.

97# Level Generation (Player-Facing)
98
99- **Circuit or Point-to-Point**
100 Circuits return to the start; point-to-point ends elsewhere.
101- **Size**
102 Width/Height of the grid. Bigger maps -> more complex tracks.
103- **Steps**
104 Higher steps carve longer/more varied routes.
105- **Preview**
106 A pixel-perfect preview texture is generated so you can verify before saving.
107
108> Under the hood: the generator uses BFS “flooding” to carve a drivable road and ensures spacing so branches don’t crowd.

123# Tips & Driving
124
125- **Feather the steering** don’t just hold it.
126- **Handbrake** helps pivot the car on tight hairpins—use sparingly.
127- **Watch the minimap / road** color in the preview to predict corners.
128- **Gamepad** offers smoother steering at speed thanks to analog input.

132# Troubleshooting
133
134- **No Input:**
135 Check that your Input Actions are bound. If using defaults, the game will auto-create bindings. Tap any key/gamepad to re-detect the device.
136
137- **No Sound:**
138 Ensure an AudioListener exists (usually on the main camera) and that volumes aren’t set to 0 in settings.
139
140- **Low FPS with Large Tracks:**
141 Try smaller grid sizes or fewer steps when generating.
142
143- **Missing Levels in Browser:**
144 Ensure you saved/kept the level from the popup after preview.
